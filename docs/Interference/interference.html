Interference

This script simulates the time evolution of two wave packets moving towards and past eachother. It is meant to visualize constructive interference. \Psi is a linear combination of two wavepackets, \Psi_1 and \Psi_2. As per the Born rule \Psi^2 gives us the probability density, \Psi^2 is therefore drawn as the dominant visual element of the visualization. Users will likely notice, when the two wavepackets interfere they don't combine to a simply larger 'lump' but we get a sinusoidal looking wavefunction. The real part of the total wavefunction is drawn underneath in white to hint to why that might be.
