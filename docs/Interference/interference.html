Interference

This script simulates the time evolution of two wave packets moving towards, and past eachother. It is meant to visualize constructive interference. \Psi is a linear combination of two wavepackets, \Psi_1 and \Psi_2. As per the Born rule \Psi^2 gives us the probability density, \Psi^2 is therefore drawn as the dominant visual element of the visualization. Users will likely notice, when the two wavepackets interfere they don't combine to a simply larger 'lump', we get a sinusoidal looking wavefunction. The real part of the total wavefunction is drawn underneath, in white, to hint to why that might be.

Users interact with a and b, the coefficients of the individual wave packets \Psi = a \Psi_1 + b \Psi_2 . Interaction is direct for a (squared) and is set by moving a slider from 1 to 0. Since each packet is normalized and the total wavefunction must be normalized as well, setting the value of a indirectly sets the value of b.

The only other elements of the visualization is the equation:
\Psi = a \Psi_1 + b \Psi_2
along with a legend, telling the user what the red and white lines represent.
