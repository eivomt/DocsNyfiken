Bound states

This visualization shows the time evolution of a wave function represented as a superposition of the bound states of a potential well. The user can adjust the probability associated with each of the six available energy eigenstates, thereby controlling the composition of the superposition.

At any point, the user may perform a measurement. The wave function then collapses into one of the six allowed states, with the probability of each outcome determined by the selected coefficients. While the result of any individual measurement is inherently unpredictable, the distribution of outcomes over many measurements follows the underlying probability distribution.

To illustrate this, every measurement is recorded and displayed in a histogram alongside the probability controls. As the number of measurements increases, the measured distribution gradually converges towards the probabilities specified by the user, demonstrating the statistical interpretation of quantum mechanics and the Born rule.

A convenience option is provided to perform five measurements simultaneously. This allows the distribution to emerge more quickly once the behaviour of individual wave function collapses has been observed.

The visualization is implemented using p5.js and follows the standard p5.js execution model. The setup() function is executed once when the page loads and initializes the simulation, while draw() is executed once per frame and continuously updates the visualization.
