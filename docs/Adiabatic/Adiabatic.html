Adiabatic

This simulation models the time evolution of a wave packet initially prepared in an energy eigenstate of a potential well. The user can move the well using the red control point, causing the wave function to evolve in response to the changing potential. The particle mass can also be adjusted, allowing the effect of mass on the system's dynamical behaviour to be explored.

A central objective of the visualization is to demonstrate the principle of adiabatic evolution: a quantum system can remain in the same eigenstate even when the potential changes with time, provided that the change occurs sufficiently slowly. To make this behaviour easier to observe, the simulation includes an automatic mode that moves the potential well at a controlled rate, as reproducing such motion manually can be difficult.

The automatic mode can also be used to investigate the approximate limit at which adiabatic behaviour breaks down. By gradually increasing the speed of the potential well and observing when the wave function begins to deviate noticeably from its eigenstate, the user can obtain a qualitative estimate of the maximum rate of change that still preserves adiabatic evolution.
