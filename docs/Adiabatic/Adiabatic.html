Adiabatic

This script simulates the time evolution of a wave packet, initially in a stable eigenstate in a potential well. The user interacts with a little red button to move the well around and the wavefunction evolves according to the change in the potential wells position. Users can also change the mass of the particle represented by the wave function and experience the effect of a different mass on the particles inertia. A core learning objective of this script is to show how the wave function can remain stable even when the potentiall wells position changes in time, so long as its rate of change is slow enough. To that end, the simulation includes support for automatically moving the potential well, as moving it by hand in a controlled fashion is difficult with the controls given.
