Line Spectrum

This visualization illustrates the emission spectrum of hydrogen. When an electron transitions from a higher energy level to a lower one, the atom emits a photon whose wavelength is determined by the difference between the two energy levels.

Interaction

The user selects an initial and final energy level. If the selected transition is valid, the corresponding wavelength is calculated and displayed on screen. A sinusoidal wave is also drawn to represent the emitted photon, with its frequency shown relative to the selected wavelength.

If the wavelength lies within the visible range, approximately 380–780 nm, a coloured spectral line is added to the visible spectrum bar. If the wavelength falls outside this range, the visualization instead highlights either the ultraviolet or infrared indicator, depending on the result.
