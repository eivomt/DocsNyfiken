Line spectrum

Visualizes the emission spectrum of hydrogen.
Users interact by choosing what energy levels to decay from and to at which point the corresponding wavelength is written to screen along with a sinus wave drawn to screen with a relative corresponding frequency. If the wavelength is within ~380 - 780 nm, a line, of that color, is drawn on a black bar representing the whole visible spectrum. If not UV or IR does a little feedback move, to indicate a wavelength in the ultraviolet or infrared according to what wavelength was recorded.
