User interaction

The user interacts with the visualization by selecting an initial energy level and a lower final energy level. When a valid downward transition is chosen, the program calculates the corresponding photon wavelength and displays the associated electromagnetic wave.

Each clickable energy line stores its quantum number in data-n.

When the line is clicked, the script reads this value as nFrom and compares it to the currently selected final level, nTo. A transition is only shown if nTo < nFrom, corresponding to emission from a higher to a lower energy state.

The emitted wavelength is then found from waveLengthArray. The matching SVG wave is displayed and animated, while the transition arrow moves from the initial level to the final level. If the wavelength lies outside the visible range, the infrared or ultraviolet label is briefly highlighted.

After the animation, the selected transition and wavelength are written to the interface, for example as a transition from n = 4 to n = 2 with its corresponding wavelength in nanometres.
